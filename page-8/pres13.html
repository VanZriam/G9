The 13th President: Joseph Estrada

Joseph Estrada

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia (summarized)

Joseph Ejercito Estrada, also known as Erap, is a Filipino actor and politician who served as the thirteenth president of the Philippines, from 1998 to 2001. A popular film star, he was ousted in 2001 amid corruption allegations.

Early life and career

Born Jose Marcelo Ejercito, Estrada became a leading film actor. He was Mayor of San Juan (1969-1986), a Senator (1987-1992) and Vice President under Fidel V. Ramos (1992-1998).

Presidency

Estrada won the 1998 election in a landslide on a pro-poor platform. In 2000, jueteng payoff allegations led to his impeachment by the House of Representatives. After the Senate trial collapsed in January 2001, mass protests known as EDSA II forced him from office on January 20, 2001.

Later life

Estrada was convicted of plunder in 2007 and sentenced to life imprisonment, but was pardoned by President Arroyo the same year. He ran again in 2010, and later served as Mayor of Manila from 2013 to 2019.
