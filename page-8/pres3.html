The 3rd President: Jose P. Laurel

Jose P. Laurel

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia (summarized)

Jose Paciano Laurel y García was a Filipino lawyer, jurist and politician who served as president of the Japanese-sponsored Second Philippine Republic from 1943 to 1945. He is also remembered as an Associate Justice of the Supreme Court and a Senator.

Early life and career

A graduate of the University of the Philippines and Yale Law School, Laurel served as Secretary of the Interior and as Associate Justice of the Supreme Court (1936-1942) before the outbreak of the war.

Presidency

During the Japanese occupation, Laurel headed the commission that drafted the 1943 Constitution and was elected president of the Second Republic on October 14, 1943. His government faced severe wartime shortages, and the republic was dissolved in August 1945.

Later life and death

Laurel was imprisoned in Japan and then in the Philippines for collaboration, but was granted amnesty in 1948. He was elected Senator in 1951 and helped negotiate the Laurel-Langley Agreement in 1955. He died in Manila on November 6, 1959.
