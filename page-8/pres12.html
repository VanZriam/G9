The 12th President: Fidel V. Ramos

Fidel V. Ramos

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia (summarized)

Fidel Valdez Ramos, also known as FVR, was a Filipino general and politician who served as the twelfth president of the Philippines, from 1992 to 1998. He is regarded as one of the country's most effective leaders, credited with economic reform and political stability.

Early life and career

A graduate of the United States Military Academy at West Point, Ramos served in the Korean and Vietnam Wars and led the Philippine Constabulary. He supported the People Power Revolution in 1986 and later served as Chief of Staff of the Armed Forces and Secretary of National Defense.

Presidency

Elected with a plurality in 1992, Ramos pursued deregulation and liberalization under his 'Philippines 2000' program, ended the long power crisis, and signed a peace agreement with the Moro National Liberation Front in 1996. The Philippines weathered the 1997 Asian financial crisis better than many neighbors.

Later life and death

After his term, Ramos remained active as an elder statesman. He died of complications from COVID-19 on July 31, 2022, at the age of 94.
