The 6th President: Elpidio Quirino

Elpidio Quirino

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia (summarized)

Elpidio Rivera Quirino was a Filipino lawyer and politician who served as the sixth president of the Philippines, from 1948 to 1953. He took office after the death of Manuel Roxas and later won a full term in 1949.

Early life and career

Quirino served in the House and Senate, and as Secretary of Finance and Secretary of State. He became Vice President in 1946 under Roxas. In 1945, his wife and three of his children were killed during the Battle of Manila.

Presidency

Quirino focused on postwar reconstruction and economic development, and offered amnesty to the Huks. He sent a Philippine expeditionary force to the Korean War in 1950 and signed a mutual defense treaty with the United States in 1951. His administration was marred by corruption allegations and a disputed 1949 election.

Later life and death

Quirino lost the 1953 election to Ramon Magsaysay. He died of a heart attack on February 29, 1956.
