The 14th President: Gloria Macapagal Arroyo

Gloria Macapagal Arroyo

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia (summarized)

Maria Gloria Macaraeg Macapagal Arroyo, often called GMA, is a Filipino economist and politician who served as the fourteenth president of the Philippines, from 2001 to 2010. She is the daughter of former president Diosdado Macapagal and the second female president of the country.

Early life and career

Arroyo earned a doctorate in economics and taught at Ateneo de Manila University before joining the government as an Undersecretary of Trade and Industry. She served as Senator (1992-1998) and Vice President (1998-2001) under Estrada.

Presidency

Arroyo assumed the presidency after EDSA II in 2001 and won a full term in 2004. Her administration achieved steady economic growth and abolished the death penalty in 2006, but was shadowed by the 2004 'Hello Garci' election controversy and corruption allegations. The 2009 Maguindanao massacre occurred late in her term.

Later career

After her presidency, Arroyo was elected to the House of Representatives representing Pampanga in 2010 and served as Speaker of the House from 2018 to 2019. She was acquitted of plunder by the Supreme Court in 2016.
