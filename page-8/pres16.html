The 16th President: Rodrigo Duterte

Rodrigo Duterte

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia (summarized)

Rodrigo Roa Duterte is a Filipino lawyer and politician who served as the sixteenth president of the Philippines, from 2016 to 2022. He is the first president from Mindanao and is known for his controversial 'war on drugs.'

Early life and career

Duterte became a lawyer and prosecutor in Davao City. He served as its Mayor for more than two decades, and as a member of the House of Representatives (1998-2001), earning a reputation for strict anti-crime policies.

Presidency

Duterte won the 2016 election on a platform of fighting crime and drugs. His anti-drug campaign led to thousands of deaths, drawing international criticism. His administration declared martial law in Mindanao after the 2017 Marawi siege, pursued the 'Build, Build, Build' infrastructure program, signed the Bangsamoro Organic Law, and led the pandemic response.

After the presidency

Duterte was arrested on March 11, 2025 on an International Criminal Court warrant and transferred to The Hague. All three counts of crimes against humanity were confirmed on April 23, 2026, and his trial is scheduled to open on November 30, 2026. He won the Davao City mayoralty in May 2025 while in detention.
