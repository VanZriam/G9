The 1st President: Emilio Aguinaldo

Emilio Aguinaldo

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia (summarized)

Emilio Famy Aguinaldo y Famy was a Filipino revolutionary and politician who served as the first president of the Philippines (1899-1901) and as leader of the First Philippine Republic. He led the Philippine forces against Spain and later against the United States.

Early life and revolution

Born in Kawit, Cavite, Aguinaldo became the town's capitan municipal in 1895 and joined the Katipunan, leading its Magdalo faction. After the 1897 Pact of Biak-na-Bato he went into exile in Hong Kong, returned in May 1898, and declared Philippine independence at Kawit on June 12, 1898.

Presidency

The First Philippine Republic was inaugurated at Malolos on January 23, 1899, under the Malolos Constitution. War with the United States broke out on February 4, 1899. Aguinaldo was captured by American forces at Palanan, Isabela on March 23, 1901 and swore allegiance to the United States on April 1.

Later life and death

Aguinaldo lost the 1935 presidential election to Manuel L. Quezon, and later promoted veterans' causes. He died of coronary thrombosis on February 6, 1964, at the age of 94.
