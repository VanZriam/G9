The 11th President: Corazon Aquino

Corazon Aquino

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia (summarized)

Maria Corazon Sumulong Cojuangco Aquino was a Filipino politician who served as the eleventh president of the Philippines, from 1986 to 1992. She was the first female president of the Philippines and the first woman president in Asia.

Early life and rise

Educated in New York, Aquino was a housewife and the wife of opposition leader Benigno Aquino Jr. After his assassination in 1983, she became the face of the opposition and ran against Marcos in the 1986 snap election.

Presidency

Aquino took office after the People Power Revolution. Her administration ratified the 1987 Constitution, restored democratic institutions, and launched the Comprehensive Agrarian Reform Program (1988) and the Local Government Code (1991). Her government survived several coup attempts and the 1991 eruption of Mount Pinatubo.

Later life and death

Aquino did not seek re-election in 1992. She remained a respected moral figure and died of colon cancer on August 1, 2009, at the age of 76.
