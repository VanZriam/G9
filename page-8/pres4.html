The 4th President: Sergio Osmeña

Sergio Osmeña

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia (summarized)

Sergio Osmeña Sr. was a Filipino lawyer and politician who served as the fourth president of the Philippines and the second of the Commonwealth, from 1944 to 1946. He is known as the 'Grand Old Man of Cebu.'

Early life and career

Osmeña was Governor of Cebu and became the first Speaker of the Philippine Assembly in 1907, a position he held until 1922. He later served in the Senate and as Vice President of the Commonwealth under Quezon (1935-1944).

Presidency

Osmeña became president when Quezon died on August 1, 1944. He landed at Leyte with General Douglas MacArthur on October 20, 1944 and re-established the Commonwealth government on Philippine soil as the country was being liberated.

Later life and death

Osmeña lost the 1946 election to Manuel Roxas. He retired from politics and died in Quezon City on October 19, 1961.
