The 9th President: Diosdado Macapagal

Diosdado Macapagal

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia (summarized)

Diosdado Pangan Macapagal Sr. was a Filipino lawyer and politician who served as the ninth president of the Philippines, from 1961 to 1965. He rose from a poor family in Pampanga and is the father of future president Gloria Macapagal Arroyo.

Early life and career

Macapagal worked his way through law school at the University of Santo Tomas and topped the bar exam. He served in the House of Representatives from 1949 to 1957 and as Vice President under Carlos P. Garcia (1957-1961).

Presidency

Macapagal moved the celebration of Independence Day from July 4 to June 12 in 1962. His administration enacted the Agricultural Land Reform Code (1963), lifted exchange controls, and revived the Philippine claim to North Borneo (Sabah).

Later life and death

Macapagal lost the 1965 election to Ferdinand Marcos. He later chaired the 1971 Constitutional Convention and died on April 21, 1997, at the age of 86.
