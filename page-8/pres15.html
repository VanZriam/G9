The 15th President: Benigno Aquino III

Benigno Aquino III

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia (summarized)

Benigno Simeon Cojuangco Aquino III, also known as Noynoy or PNoy, was a Filipino politician who served as the fifteenth president of the Philippines, from 2010 to 2016. He was the son of Benigno Aquino Jr. and Corazon Aquino.

Early life and career

Aquino studied economics at Ateneo de Manila University and served in the House of Representatives (1998-2007) and the Senate (2007-2010). He ran for president in 2010 after the death of his mother and won on an anti-corruption platform.

Presidency

Under his 'Daang Matuwid' (Straight Path) campaign, the economy grew strongly and the country reached investment-grade credit ratings. His administration enacted the Reproductive Health Law (2012) and the K-12 education program, signed the Bangsamoro peace framework, and filed the arbitration case against China, which the Philippines won in 2016. Controversies included the 2015 Mamasapano clash.

Later life and death

After leaving office, Aquino lived privately. He died of kidney disease secondary to diabetes on June 24, 2021, at the age of 61.
