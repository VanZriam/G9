The 5th President: Manuel Roxas

Manuel Roxas

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia (summarized)

Manuel Acuña Roxas was a Filipino politician who served as the fifth president of the Philippines, from 1946 until his death in 1948. He was the last president of the Commonwealth and the first of the independent Third Republic.

Early life and career

A lawyer from Capiz, Roxas served as Governor of Capiz, Speaker of the House of Representatives (1922-1933) and later as Senate President. He founded the Liberal Party after breaking with the Nacionalistas in 1946.

Presidency

Roxas won the 1946 election and presided over Philippine independence on July 4, 1946. His administration signed the Bell Trade Act and the Military Bases Agreement with the United States, granted amnesty to wartime collaborators, and struggled with the Hukbalahap rebellion.

Death

Roxas died of a heart attack on April 15, 1948, after delivering a speech at Clark Air Base. Vice President Elpidio Quirino succeeded him.
