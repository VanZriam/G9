The 8th President: Carlos P. Garcia

Carlos P. Garcia

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia (summarized)

Carlos Polestico Garcia was a Filipino teacher, poet and politician who served as the eighth president of the Philippines, from 1957 to 1961. He became president after the death of Ramon Magsaysay and won a full term later that year.

Early life and career

A teacher and lawyer known as the 'Bard of Bohol,' Garcia served as Governor of Bohol and Senator. He was Vice President (1953-1957) under Magsaysay and concurrently Secretary of Foreign Affairs.

Presidency

Garcia's administration is best known for the 'Filipino First' policy, which favored Filipino businesses over foreign ones, and an austerity program to fight corruption. In 1959 the Bohlen-Serrano Agreement shortened the lease of American bases to 25 years.

Later life and death

Garcia lost the 1961 election to Diosdado Macapagal. He later served in the 1971 Constitutional Convention and died on June 14, 1971.
