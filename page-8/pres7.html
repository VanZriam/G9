The 7th President: Ramon Magsaysay

Ramon Magsaysay

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia (summarized)

Ramon del Fierro Magsaysay Sr. was a Filipino statesman who served as the seventh president of the Philippines, from 1953 until his death in 1957. Popularly called the 'Champion of the Masses,' he remains one of the country's most admired leaders.

Early life and career

A mechanic and bus company manager before the war, Magsaysay became a guerrilla leader against the Japanese and was appointed military governor of Zambales. He served in Congress (1946-1950) and as Secretary of National Defense (1950-1953), where he led the campaign against the Huk rebellion.

Presidency

Magsaysay won the 1953 election in a landslide. He opened Malacañang to the public, pursued land reform, and promoted clean government. In 1954 the Philippines joined the Southeast Asia Treaty Organization (SEATO) under his administration.

Death and legacy

Magsaysay died in a plane crash on Mount Manunggal, Cebu, on March 17, 1957. The Ramon Magsaysay Award, often called Asia's Nobel Prize, was established in his memory.
