The 17th President: Bongbong Marcos

Bongbong Marcos

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia (summarized)

Ferdinand 'Bongbong' Romualdez Marcos Jr. is a Filipino politician serving as the seventeenth president of the Philippines since 2022. He is the son of former president Ferdinand Marcos and Imelda Marcos.

Early life and career

Marcos served as Governor of Ilocos Norte, Representative of its 2nd district, and as Senator (2010-2016). He lost the 2016 vice-presidential race to Leni Robredo by a narrow margin.

Presidency

Marcos won the 2022 election with a majority of about 58.8 percent and took office on June 30, 2022. His administration, under the 'Bagong Pilipinas' banner, has focused on economic recovery, infrastructure, and the Maharlika Investment Fund, while strengthening ties with the United States and Japan amid tensions with China in the West Philippine Sea.

Recent developments

The alliance between Marcos and Vice President Sara Duterte collapsed during his term, leading to impeachment proceedings against her. His administration also faced scrutiny over a flood-control corruption scandal, and the arrest of former president Rodrigo Duterte on an ICC warrant took place in March 2025.
