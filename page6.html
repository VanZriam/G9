The Starry Night

Vincent van Gogh · oil on canvas · 1889

The Scene

Painted from memory at the asylum in Saint-Rémy, the view looks out over a sleeping village toward rolling hills and a swirling sky, with an imagined cypress standing watch in the foreground.

The Technique

Thick, rhythmic strokes of paint build the sky into motion. The turbulence of the brushwork contrasts with the stillness of the village below it.

The Palette

Cool blues and greens dominate the canvas, broken by bursts of warm yellow in the stars and moon. The contrast gives the sky its glow and its restlessness.

Where It Was Made

Van Gogh painted it in June 1889 while a patient at the Saint-Paul-de-Mausole asylum, working from the view outside his window before sunrise.

Why It Endures

More than a century later, its restless sky still feels like a private, sleepless thought turned into color and light, and it remains one of the most recognized paintings in the world.
