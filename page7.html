THE GAME OF BASKETBALL

History · Significance · Modern Impact

Vanriam M. Cansilao 9 – Galatians

From a peach basket nailed to a gym wall to arenas watched by billions, basketball has grown into one of the most influential games on earth.

Where It All Began

Basketball was invented in December 1891 by Dr. James Naismith, who nailed two peach baskets to a gym balcony and wrote thirteen simple rules to keep his students active in winter. Backboards, nets, and dribbling were added soon after, and the game spread through YMCAs and colleges into the earliest professional leagues.

A Global Rise

All basketball needs is a ball and a hoop, which is why it spread so fast. It became an Olympic sport in 1936, the NBA was founded in 1946, and the 1992 "Dream Team" introduced the world to NBA stars on live TV. The WNBA followed in 1996. Today the game is played on courts everywhere, from city parks to backyard hoops.

The Modern Era

Basketball today is a global culture as much as a sport, streamed to millions, shaped by analytics, and tied to fashion and music. Franchises like the Lakers, home to Magic Johnson, Kobe Bryant, and LeBron James, show how the game keeps evolving while still teaching the same lessons: discipline, teamwork, and showing up for the people beside you.
